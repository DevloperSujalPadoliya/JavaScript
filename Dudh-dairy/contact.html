Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

What are your delivery hours?

We deliver from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM on weekdays and 7:00 AM to 2:00 PM on Sundays.

Is there a minimum order value?

No minimum order. However, free delivery is available on orders above ₹500.

Can I customize my milk subscription?

Yes! You can choose delivery frequency, quantity, and milk type according to your needs.

What areas do you deliver to?

We currently deliver across Bangalore. Contact us to check delivery availability in your area.

How can I return a product?

If you're not satisfied, please contact us within 24 hours with photos of the issue. We'll arrange a replacement or refund.
