Our Story

Welcome to Dudh Dairy, where tradition meets quality. Founded in 2010, we started with a simple mission: to provide families with pure, fresh, and healthy dairy products.

What began as a small family dairy farm has grown into Bangalore's most trusted name for premium dairy products. Our commitment to quality has never wavered - we still source our milk from local farmers who follow the highest standards of animal care and dairy practices.

Every product that leaves our facility is a testament to our dedication. From the moment our cows are milked to the time your order arrives at your doorstep, we ensure the highest standards of hygiene and freshness.
